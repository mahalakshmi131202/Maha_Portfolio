PROJECT / 01 · POWER BI

Customer Bike Purchase Analysis

Analyzed bike purchasing patterns across 1,000 customers. Used Power Query to prepare the data and DAX measures to compare purchase rates across demographics, regions, and commuting habits.

1,000 Customers
481 Bike buyers
48.10% Overall purchase rate
Key findings
  • Pacific customers had a 58.85% purchase rate—10.75 percentage points above the overall rate.
  • Customers commuting 2–5 miles had the highest commute-group purchase rate at 58.64%.
  • Customers with no cars had a 61.76% purchase rate, compared with 35.59% for customers with four cars.

PROJECT / 02 · SQL & POWER BI

Global COVID-19 Impact Analysis

Used SQL to explore COVID-19 cases, deaths, and vaccination data. Built a Power BI dashboard to compare countries and examine trends across the selected reporting period.

Key findings
  • The dashboard shows approximately 151 million global cases and a 2.11% death-to-case percentage.
  • Andorra had the highest recorded infection rate among the countries shown.
  • The United States had the highest recorded death count among the countries shown.

Historical dashboard period: January 1, 2020 – April 30, 2021.
